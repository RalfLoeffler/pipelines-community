# Australian Imaging Service - UNSW RINSW Pydra Workflows

Community-contributed Pydra workflows for the Australian Imaging Service by
Research Imaging NSW (RINSW) at the University of New South Wales.

This package supplies the ProtocolQC XNAT pipeline at
`australianimagingservice.community.au.edu.unsw.rinsw.protocol_qc`. Its
canonical Pydra2App specification is
`../../specs/australian-imaging-service-community-unsw/au/edu/unsw/rinsw/protocolqc.yaml`.
